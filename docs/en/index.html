About Us

Your trusted partner in electromechanical works and contracting

Wall of Art began operations in 1439 AH in Riyadh, with a commercial registration issued by the Ministry of Commerce, to meet the need for electromechanical works in particular and contracting in general.

We work under the motto “Quality in execution and speed in delivery”, through a specialized engineering and technical team, and wide relationships with agents and suppliers that ensure supply accuracy, after-sales service and warranty — contributing to Saudi Vision 2030.

Our team comprises qualified engineers and technicians with broad field experience, delivering commercial, residential, industrial and infrastructure projects with full commitment to occupational safety standards and quality systems — sourcing from approved agents and suppliers that ensures compliance, warranty and after-delivery support.

Our Vision

To expand our work across all regions of the Kingdom with high quality and efficiency, and to be among the companies contributing to Vision 2030.

Our Mission

To satisfy all our clients by executing works to the highest quality standards and technical specifications, with honest cooperation and commitment to deadlines.

Our Services

Integrated electromechanical solutions

We cover your project from design to supply and installation, then commissioning and testing — through a range of specialized systems.

01

HVAC Systems

Design, supply, installation and commissioning of all AC types: split, central, chillers (air/water-cooled), and air handling units.

02

Fire Fighting & Alarm

Early fire-fighting and alarm systems compliant with Civil Defense requirements, with UL Listed and FM Approved products, and gas suppression systems (FM200).

03

Electrical & Low-Current

Lighting networks, cabling and panels, CCTV surveillance, access control, communications and low-current systems.

04

Infrastructure Works

Water, sewage and electrical conduits using HDPE and other pipes, coordinated with architectural and structural works.

05

Pump Works

Supply, installation and commissioning of all pump types, and execution of pump rooms and accessories for water and infrastructure projects.

Our Methodology

Three integrated phases in every project

We follow a clear, disciplined methodology across all our systems — from the first drawing to final handover.

1

Design

Preparing all necessary drawings and studying the project by building type and use, with schedules and inter-department coordination.

2

Supply & Installation

Carrying out installations with specialized technicians under engineering supervision, supplying high-quality materials via our agent and supplier network.

3

Commissioning & Testing

Inspecting and testing all devices and systems to ensure quality and harmony with other works, then initial and final handover.
